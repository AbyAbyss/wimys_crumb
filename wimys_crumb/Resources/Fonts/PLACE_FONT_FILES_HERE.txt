Drop the bundled font files here (one weight per file):

  BricolageGrotesque-Medium.ttf
  BricolageGrotesque-SemiBold.ttf
  BricolageGrotesque-Bold.ttf
  BricolageGrotesque-ExtraBold.ttf
  Geist-Regular.otf
  Geist-Medium.otf
  Geist-SemiBold.otf
  Geist-Bold.otf
  GeistMono-Regular.otf
  GeistMono-Medium.otf
  GeistMono-SemiBold.otf

They are registered at launch by Theme.registerFonts() (any .ttf/.otf/.ttc
in this folder is picked up via CTFontManagerRegisterFontsForURLs).
Until the files are present, macOS falls back to the system font.

Both Bricolage Grotesque and Geist / Geist Mono are SIL OFL — bundle the
license files alongside the fonts.
